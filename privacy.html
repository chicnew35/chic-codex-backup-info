Privacy policy

Chic Codex Backup is used by the backup account owner. The Windows workflow encrypts selected local Codex conversation archives before sending them to the owner’s Google Drive account.

Google Drive access

The workflow requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. It uses the Drive API to create its backup folder, upload encrypted archives, list backup files, and download archives for integrity checks. It does not read unrelated Drive files.

Storage and sharing

Encrypted archives are stored in the owner’s Drive backup folder. The OAuth configuration and operational logs stay on the backup computer. The encryption key is kept separately. The workflow does not sell or share Google user data with third parties, use it for advertising, or use it for model training. This information site does not receive backup contents, keys, or Google Drive credentials.

Retention and control

The account owner controls which archives to retain and can remove them from Google Drive. To stop Drive access, the owner can revoke Chic Codex Backup in their Google Account connections.

Contact

Use the support contact shown on the app’s Google consent screen for privacy questions.
